About

A trusted advisor for life's biggest moves.

Peyton Yen is a New York City real estate advisor with over $100 million in closed transactions and a specialty in new development condominiums across Manhattan and Brooklyn.

Her work spans some of the city's most notable projects — including 121 East 22nd, The Brooklyn Grove, 110 N 1st, and 561 Pacific. Whether guiding a first-time buyer, an international client, or a seasoned investor, Peyton brings comprehensive market knowledge, genuine enthusiasm, and a relentless work ethic to every transaction.

Born in Taipei and raised in Shanghai, Peyton is fluent in both English and Mandarin — bridging cultures for clients navigating the New York market from near and far.
